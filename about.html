Our Story

AutoElite Motors was founded in 1994 with a singular mission: to bring world-class automobiles to every passionate driver. Starting as a small showroom in Kathmandu, we've grown into one of South Asia's most trusted automotive dealers.

Our philosophy has always been simple — put the customer first, stand behind our vehicles, and never compromise on quality. Over three decades, that philosophy has earned us 15,000+ loyal customers and numerous industry awards.

Today, AutoElite represents over 12 global brands, operates 5 service centers, and continues to expand our electric vehicle offerings as the industry evolves.
